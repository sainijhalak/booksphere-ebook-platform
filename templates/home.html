Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on accessing our catalog, saving bookmarks, and submitting reviews.

How do I access free eBooks on BookSphere?

Simply navigate to our E-Books page. You can search across titles, authors, and categories, read sample excerpts online, or download copies directly.

Can I save books to my personal reading list?

Yes! Register or sign in to your free account, then click the heart/bookmark icon on any book to add it directly to your Library.

How does the community review and rating system work?

Verified readers can post 1-to-5 star ratings and detailed critical assessments directly on each book's detail page.
