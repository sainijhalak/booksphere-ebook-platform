Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about BookSphere (OnlyBooks).

Are all eBooks on BookSphere free to access?

Yes! We believe in accessible digital literature. All curated eBooks in our primary catalog can be read online or downloaded directly.

Can I submit and publish my own eBook?

Absolutely. Authenticated users and authors can click "+ Add Book" in the navigation bar to contribute to the catalog.

How do I save books to my personal reading list?

Click the heart/bookmark icon on any book. Saved items are synced to your account and accessible at any time under "My Library".

Is there a REST API available for headless developers?

Yes. You can access our public JSON endpoints at /api/books/ and /api/books/<id>/.
